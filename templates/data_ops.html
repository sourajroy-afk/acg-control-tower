What the upload will acceptNO NEED TO RENAME COLUMNS FIRST
Headers. Common export names are mapped automatically: order no, sales order, work order and job number all read as job_no; po date and order confirmation date read as order_date; basic finish, actual finish and completion date read as actual_end; grn date reads as received_date. Case, spaces and punctuation do not matter. Unknown columns are ignored, never fatal.

Dates. Day-first and ISO both work: 2026-06-14, 14-06-2026, 14/06/2026, 14.06.26, 14-Jun-2026, 14 June 2026, and Excel serial numbers.

Bad rows. A row that cannot be read is rejected on its own, with the reason and the spreadsheet line number, and the rest of the file still loads. Download the rejected rows from the history above, fix them, and re-upload just those.
