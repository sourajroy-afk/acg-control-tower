How these live figures are measured
Lead time is order date to actual dispatch. On-time delivery compares actual dispatch with the customer commitment on the order. First time right is completed stages with no rework logged. Cost of poor quality is rework cost as a share of order value, so it covers shop-floor rework only, not scrap or warranty. WIP inventory in days of supply needs the stores inventory ledger, which this upload format does not carry, so it stays unmeasured here rather than being estimated: the Control Tower instead reports WIP value on the floor ({{ g_kpis.wip_value_lakh }} ₹ L, {{ g_kpis.wip_cover_days or '—' }} days of cover).
Risk: change fatigue
Nine levers across three years is a lot for one plant. Mitigation: run one pilot line first, publish its numbers on this tool, then scale.
Risk: data quality
Every forecast here depends on stage timestamps being entered the day they happen. Mitigation: stage sign-off in the daily huddle, and the ingest log on Data Ops shows who loaded what.
Risk: supplier resistance
VMI and long-term agreements shift inventory risk onto vendors. Mitigation: volume commitment and shared forecast in exchange, starting with the top five by spend.
Risk: capacity mirage
Freed capacity only becomes money if sales fill it. Mitigation: the simulator caps convertible capacity at 30% and the commercial team signs up to that number before capex is released.
