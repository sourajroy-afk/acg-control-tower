Reading the gap
What the plant controls now. Design, assembly and testing sit within a couple of weeks of the industry range, so standard work and slot planning close most of that gap without capital spend.
What needs suppliers. Material sourcing carries the largest single gap to best-in-class. It cannot be closed inside the plant: it needs critical item lists, vendor-managed inventory and long-term agreements.
What needs design. Fabrication variation follows from engineering variation. Modular platforms and standard BOMs are the only lever that removes it permanently, which is why they sit in Year 3.
